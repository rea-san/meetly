About Meetly

Meetly is a student project developed as part of the MSc in Interactive Digital Media at Griffith College Dublin.

The idea behind Meetly started with a simple question: what if finding someone to enjoy an activity with was just as easy as finding the activity itself?

Meetly explores how digital platforms can bring people together through shared interests, hobbies and activities. Instead of simply discovering what to do, users can discover who to do it with.

This project combines interaction design, visual communication and technology to explore new ways of creating meaningful social connections through shared experiences.
